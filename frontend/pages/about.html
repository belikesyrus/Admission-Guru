About Admission Guru

Built to help Maharashtra students navigate the complex admission process with confidence

What is Admission Guru?

Admission Guru is a free college admission predictor built specifically for Maharashtra students. Enter your entrance exam score, category, preferred branch, and other details — and get a ranked list of colleges you're realistically eligible for, based on official 2025 cutoff data published by Maharashtra CET Cell and DTE.

The tool supports MHT-CET Engineering, Direct Second Year Engineering (Diploma lateral entry), Post-SSC Diploma, B.Pharmacy, and Direct Second Year Pharmacy admissions via the CAP (Centralized Admission Process) rounds.

How the Prediction Works

🔍
Filter by your inputs

Your category, branch, round, gender, and college type are matched against the cutoff dataset.

📊
Compare with cutoffs

Your percentile is compared with the closing cutoff for each college/branch/category combination.

🏆
Rank by chance tier

Safe: Your score is 3+ percentile above cutoff. Moderate: Within 3 percentile. Ambitious: Up to 5 below cutoff.

⚠️ Disclaimer

This tool is for guidance and reference only. Cutoff data is sourced from official publications but may contain transcription errors. Admission cutoffs vary each year based on demand and seat availability. Always verify with the official Maharashtra CET Cell website and DTE portal before submitting your preference list during the actual CAP process.
